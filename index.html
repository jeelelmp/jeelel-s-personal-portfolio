About Me

Introduction

Hello, my name is Jeelel Bame Mpofu. I am an enthusiastic young adult with a keen interest in data analytics and data engineering. I have a strong passion to understand how data can be mined and transformed into meaningful information to aid decision-making and solve complex problems. I am currently enrolled at the Botswana School of Business Sciences, formally known as the Botswana Accountancy College (BAC), as a first-year student studying Business Intelligence and Data Analytics to hone my skills and work towards my goal.

Academic background

I am currently in my first year of the BSc Business Intelligence and Data Analytics programme at the Botswana School of Business Sciences. My modules include Computer Technology, Fundamentals of Business Finance, Statistical Foundations for Data Analytics, and Communication and Study Skills. I particularly enjoy the Statistical Foundations module, as it helps me understand the mathematical reasoning behind the data insights I want to uncover.

Interests

My primary interests are Data Analysis, Web development, and Coding. I am drawn to the process of using code to solve problems, build digital tools, and make data accessible and useful for everyone.
